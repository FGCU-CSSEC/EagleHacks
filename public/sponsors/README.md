# Sponsor logos

Drop sponsor logos here as they come in (e.g. `acme.svg` or `acme.png`).

- Prefer SVG, or a transparent PNG at least 600px wide.
- Use a white/light version, since the Sponsors section is on a dark background.
- Name files in lowercase with hyphens: `company-name.svg`.
- Files here are served at `/sponsors/<file>`.
