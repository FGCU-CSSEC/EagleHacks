Build. Learn. Meet.

EagleHacks is a 36-hour hackathon at Florida Gulf Coast University. Students from FGCU and schools across Florida team up, build something new, and present it to judges from industry. Free, beginner-friendly, 18+.

Build

A project from scratch, solo or in a team of up to four.

Learn

Workshops from mentors and sponsors. No experience needed.

Meet

Recruiters and engineers from local and national companies.
